Botanical Warmth

A calm palette with a lively little accent.

Garden green and mint ground the experience; cool sky surfaces keep the interface airy, while coral adds a focused note of warmth. The supplied design tokens also define Plus Jakarta Sans for display text and Inter for interface copy.
